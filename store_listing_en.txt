FULL DESCRIPTION
--------------------------------------
Untangle Rings is a calm, satisfying puzzle game about sorting out a tangled mess of overlapping rings - one level at a time.

Drag each ring into place until nothing overlaps and the board is clear. Simple to learn, relaxing to play, and just tricky enough to keep your mind engaged.

FEATURES
- Hundreds of hand-crafted levels, from easy warm-ups to brain-bending tangles
- Earn coins every time you complete a level
- Stuck? Use a Hint to reveal the next move
- Spend coins in the Shop for hints and extra rewards
- Clean, colorful visuals with smooth, soothing animations
- Simple settings to control sound and music
- No timers, no pressure - play at your own pace

Whether you have five minutes to spare or an hour to unwind, Untangle Rings is the perfect low-stress puzzle to pick up anytime, anywhere.
